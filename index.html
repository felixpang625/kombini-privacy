Privacy Policy for Kombini

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Contact: felixpang625@gmail.com

Kombini is a personal health-and-performance tool. It pulls your own wearable data together with entries you make yourself, and builds models and experiments on top of them so you can learn how your body and attention actually respond to what you do. This policy explains exactly what Kombini accesses, why, where it is stored, and what control you have.

Plain-language summary. Kombini reads your WHOOP data with your permission, combines it with things you log yourself, and stores the result in your own database. It does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with third parties for their own purposes. You can export or delete everything at any time.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Kombini application and any companion tools (including the self-report bot and cognitive task) that connect to your account. It does not cover WHOOP's own apps and services, which are governed by WHOOP's privacy policy.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data from connected services (with your explicit authorisation)

Kombini connects to the official WHOOP API using OAuth 2.0. You approve each data category during authorisation, and access is read-only — Kombini cannot post to, or change, your WHOOP account.

CategoryExamples
Recovery & cardiovascularRecovery score, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, skin temperature, blood oxygen
SleepSleep onset and wake times, sleep duration, sleep stages, sleep performance and consistency, sleep need and debt
Strain & activityDay strain, workout strain, workout heart rate, energy expenditure, activity type and timing
Profile & bodyBasic profile identifiers and body measurements (e.g. height, weight)

If you connect any other provider, Kombini accesses only the categories you approve, and they are listed in the table above by equivalence.

2.2 Data you enter yourself

  • Self-reports — mood, focus, perceived stress, alcohol, caffeine timing, screen time, and any free-text notes you choose to add.
  • Cognitive task results — reaction times, lapse counts and related metrics from the Psychomotor Vigilance Task.

2.3 Optional data you may choose to add

  • Neurophysiological signals (e.g. EEG band powers) if you connect a headset.
  • Continuous glucose data if you connect a sensor.
  • Laboratory results you enter manually.

These are entirely optional and only collected if you switch them on.

2.4 Derived data

Kombini generates predictions, summaries, effect estimates and experiment results from the data above. This derived data is personal data, is treated exactly like its inputs, and never leaves your database without your action.

2.5 What we do not collect

Kombini does not collect your contacts, photos, location history, or the contents of your messages beyond the self-report entries you deliberately send to the logging channel. It does not read game or application memory, and it does not run in the background of other software.

3. How we use your data

Kombini uses your data only to:

  • display your own metrics back to you;
  • fit and evaluate personal forecasting models;
  • run randomised single-subject (n-of-1) experiments you design, and report the results;
  • maintain and debug the application.

Legal bases (where GDPR applies). We process this data on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) and, for health-related data, your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)). You may withdraw consent at any time; see Section 7. Kombini does not use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Where your data is stored

Kombini is local-first. Your records are stored in a database on your own device (or, where you deploy it, on a server you control), encrypted at rest. No copy is sent to us or to any third party as part of normal operation.

If you choose to use a hosted version of Kombini, that deployment is described to you before you use it, including its hosting location and retention period.

5. Sharing and disclosure

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share it for advertising or profiling.
  • We disclose data only if you explicitly instruct us to, or where we are legally compelled (for example, by valid legal process), in which case we will tell you unless prohibited.
  • Service providers. If you use optional features, the following processors may handle data on your instruction:
    • WHOOP — source of wearable data (you authorise this directly with WHOOP).
    • Discord — transport only, if you use the self-report bot. Messages you send to the logging channel are relayed to your database. Discord's own handling is governed by Discord's privacy policy.
    • Your hosting/LLM provider — only if you enable an optional feature that sends data to it, and only the minimum required.

6. Retention

Data is retained for as long as you keep it. Kombini does not impose a retention limit of its own. You may delete any record, any date range, or your entire database at any time (Section 7). Backups, if you enable them, are deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

Subject to applicable law (including the GDPR and the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance), you have the right to:

  • Access your data and receive a copy in a portable format;
  • Export your full dataset at any time (machine-readable export is built in);
  • Rectify inaccurate entries;
  • Erase individual records or your entire account and database;
  • Restrict or object to processing;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

Because Kombini is local-first, most of these are available directly in the application. For anything else, or to disconnect a provider, email felixpang625@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Security

Data is encrypted at rest. API credentials and tokens are stored in environment configuration, not in the application's source code or database, and OAuth access can be revoked by you at any time from the provider's settings. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

9. International transfers

Normal operation keeps your data on your own device, so no transfer occurs. If you enable a hosted or third-party feature that involves a transfer outside your jurisdiction, that transfer relies on your consent and appropriate safeguards, and is disclosed to you before you enable it.

10. Children

Kombini is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has used Kombini, contact us and we will delete the data.

11. Health disclaimer

Kombini is a wellness and self-experimentation tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Metrics such as recovery scores are proprietary third-party estimates, not clinical measurements. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about any health concern, and do not use Kombini to make medical decisions.

12. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date will change and, where appropriate, we will notify you in the application or by email. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
